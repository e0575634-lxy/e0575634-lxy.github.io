Xinyu Li 李昕宇

Welcome to my website!

I am an economist with a Ph.D. in Economics from the School of Social Sciences at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), supervised by Prof. Jonathan Tan and Prof. Jubo Yan. I also hold a Master of Economics from the National University of Singapore and a bachelor's degree from Beijing Normal University.

My research interests lie in experimental economics, social preferences, organizational behavior, and information transmission. My current work uses laboratory and field experiments to study strategic communication, dishonesty, fairness, cooperation, and behavior under asymmetric information.

For readers searching by family-name order, I am also listed as Li Xinyu in some institutional records.
