Working Papers

Persuasive Lies: An Experiment on Multidimensional Communication
with Jubo Yan and Jonathan Tan

A sender–receiver cheap-talk experiment studies whether richer message spaces increase persuasive power while also expanding the scope for strategic misinformation under asymmetric information.

Fairness as an Excuse: Dishonesty Through Motivated Reasoning
with Jonathan Tan

A real-effort experiment examines whether perceived unfairness licenses misreporting through motivated reasoning and whether making the accuracy norm salient reduces moral wiggle room and cheating.

Lying to Help: Prosocial and Conditional Dishonesty in Feedback Provision
with Jonathan Tan

A laboratory experiment studies organizational feedback and self-reported performance when providers' and recipients' incentives are aligned or misaligned.

Inequality, Positive and Negative Framework and Conditional Cooperation of Rural Children—Large-scale field experiments based on public goods and bads
with Yexin Zhou, Ru Chen, and Jubo Yan

A large-scale field experiment studies children's cooperation in a public-goods game and how framing, non-cognitive skills, and family background shape conditional cooperation.
