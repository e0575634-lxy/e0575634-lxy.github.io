Working Papers

Persuasive Lies: An Experiment on Multidimensional Communication
with Jubo Yan and Jonathan Tan
A sender–receiver cheap-talk experiment models real-world third-party communication such as credit ratings and online reviews, where a partially informed sender may be aligned or misaligned with the receiver under asymmetric information. It tests whether richer message spaces increase persuasive power while also expanding scope for strategic misinformation, and examines the resulting implications for market efficiency and social welfare.
Fairness as an Excuse: Dishonesty Through Motivated Reasoning
with Jonathan Tan
Employing a real-effort ball-catching task to mimic a workplace environment, this project tests whether perceived unfairness, both objective and subjective, licenses misreporting through motivated reasoning, and whether making an accuracy norm salient reduces moral wiggle room and cheating.
Lying to Help: Prosocial and Conditional Dishonesty in Feedback Provision
with Jonathan Tan
A laboratory experiment on organizational feedback and self-reported performance. By varying whether feedback providers' and recipients' incentives are aligned or misaligned, the study examines when dishonesty can be justified by social preferences and how recipients respond as honesty norms weaken.
Inequality, Positive and Negative Framework and Conditional Cooperation of Rural Children—Large-scale Field Experiments Based on Public Goods and Bads
with Yexin Zhou, Ru Chen, and Jubo Yan
A large-scale field experiment on children's cooperation behavior in a public-goods game testing how framing, non-cognitive skills, and family background shape conditional cooperation.
